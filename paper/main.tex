\title{Exact Positive-Diagonal Matignon Stability in Dimension Three:\\
A Fractional Deformation of Cain's D-Stability Threshold}
\author{Maximiliano Lucius}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We study positive-diagonal multiplicative stability for commensurate fractional-order systems in the Matignon region. Generalized region D-stability and forbidden-boundary criteria are known, but they retain a universal quantifier over all positive diagonal multipliers. For real \(3\times3\) matrices on the full-dimensional strict-\(P(-A)\) stratum, we eliminate that multiplier exactly. Four positive-left-diagonal orbit invariants reduce the complete orbit to a two-dimensional simplex, and fractional D-stability is equivalent to the scalar inequality \(\kappa<T_\alpha(\beta)\). At integer order, \(T_\alpha\) reduces exactly to Cain's classical real-\(3\times3\) D-stability boundary; for \(2/3<\alpha<1\), the strict band between the classical and fractional thresholds is genuinely fractional and full dimensional. We prove that the threshold problem is strictly convex in logit coordinates, has a unique smooth optimizer, admits an explicit global parametrization, and has sharp asymptotics at both limiting orders. Consequently, dimension three is the smallest real dimension in which the fractional difference from classical Hurwitz D-stability has nonempty full-dimensional interior. For three-species generalized Lotka--Volterra systems, the orbit invariants become exact reciprocal-pair and directed-cycle feedback coordinates. High-precision and interval-certified computations independently corroborate the analytic results.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:}
D-stability; fractional-order systems; Matignon stability; P-matrices; positive diagonal scaling; generalized Lotka--Volterra systems.
