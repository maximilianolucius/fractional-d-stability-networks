\title{Exact Positive-Diagonal Matignon Stability in Dimension Three:\\
A Fractional Deformation of Cain's D-Stability Threshold}
\author{Maximiliano Lucius}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We study positive-diagonal multiplicative stability for commensurate fractional-order systems in the Matignon region
\[
\Sigma_\alpha=\{z\neq0:|\arg z|>\alpha\pi/2\},\qquad 0<\alpha<1.
\]
Generalized region D-stability and forbidden-boundary criteria are known, but they retain a universal quantifier over all positive diagonal multipliers. We give an explicit exact elimination of that multiplier for real \(3\times3\) matrices on the full-dimensional strict-\(P(-A)\) stratum. Four positive-left-diagonal orbit invariants \((\beta_{12},\beta_{13},\beta_{23},\kappa)\) reduce the complete orbit to a two-dimensional simplex, and fractional D-stability is equivalent to the scalar inequality
\[
\kappa<T_\alpha(\beta).
\]
At \(\alpha=1\), the threshold reduces exactly to Cain's classical real-\(3\times3\) D-stability boundary. For \(2/3<\alpha<1\), the strict band \(T_1(\beta)<\kappa<T_\alpha(\beta)\) characterizes a genuinely fractional full-dimensional region. We prove that the threshold problem is strictly convex in logit coordinates, has a unique smooth optimizer, admits an explicit global parametrization, and has sharp asymptotics at both \(\alpha\to1^-\) and \(\alpha\downarrow2/3\). As a consequence, dimension three is the smallest real dimension in which the genuinely fractional difference from classical Hurwitz D-stability has nonempty full-dimensional interior. For three-species generalized Lotka--Volterra systems, the orbit invariants become exact reciprocal-pair and directed-cycle feedback coordinates. Extensive high-precision and interval-certified computations independently corroborate the analytic results.
\end{abstract}
