\begin{abstract}
We study classes of fractional-order ecological networks rather than a single model or a fixed collection of topologies. For commensurate Caputo systems ${}^C D^\alpha x=F_G(x;\theta)$, we characterize stability through the angular geometry of the equilibrium Jacobian spectrum and isolate parameter regions in which memory stabilizes equilibria that are unstable in the corresponding first-order dynamics. The manuscript is organized to separate general structural results, graph-dependent consequences, and reproducible numerical evidence. This file is an initial repository scaffold and must be replaced or expanded by the current research manuscript before submission.
\end{abstract}
