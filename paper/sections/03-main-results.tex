\section{Main results}
\textbf{Status convention.} Every statement in this section must carry an explicit status during development: \emph{proved}, \emph{conditional}, \emph{conjectured}, or \emph{numerically supported}. Do not promote computational evidence to theorem status.

\subsection{Spectral threshold}
For a nonsingular Jacobian define
\begin{equation}
\alpha_c(J_G)=\frac{2}{\pi}\min_i |\arg\lambda_i(J_G)|.
\end{equation}
Strict fractional asymptotic stability holds whenever $\alpha<\alpha_c(J_G)$, subject to the standard commensurate Caputo assumptions.

\subsection{Fractional D-stability}
The intended structural question is stability of $D J_G$ over positive diagonal matrices $D$. Exact theorems, sufficient conditions, necessary conditions, topology-dependent obstructions, and counterexamples should be separated from finite numerical sampling.
