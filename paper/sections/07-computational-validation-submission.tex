\section{Computational certification and reproducibility}
\label{sec:computation}

All principal results are analytic. Computation was used only for adversarial falsification, interval certification, sensitivity checks, and reproducible visualization. Two independent numerical routes were compared: the invariant-space minimization defining \(T_\alpha\), and direct minimization of the spectral Matignon margin over two log-diagonal ratios using eigenvalues of the full matrix \(DA\).

The combined audit comprised \(2.18\times10^6\) adversarial matrix/order cases, \(56{,}234\) multiprecision rechecks of numerically delicate cases, a \(938\)-point threshold atlas, \(35\) canonical witness matrices, \(128{,}000\) floating-point differential checks plus \(2{,}500\) high-precision rechecks, and a final \(135/135\) passing test suite. No genuine discrepancy with Theorem~\ref{thm:C10} or the threshold geometry survived independent high-precision re-evaluation.

Of the \(938\) atlas points, \(903\) were globally certified by interval branch-and-bound without invoking Theorem~\ref{thm:strict-convexity}. For \(35\) extreme-anisotropy points, localization was completed using the already-proved strict-convexity/uniqueness result. The worst reported relative enclosure width was
\[
1.1\times10^{-35}.
\]
All independent 50-digit optimizer values lay inside their enclosures, and all \(312\) symmetric points contained the exact closed form of Corollary~\ref{cor:symmetric-slice}.

A representative nonsymmetric witness is
\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&-3/2&0\\
-1/2&-1&-3\\
-3&0&-2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \alpha=0.7.
\]
It is Hurwitz at \(D=I\), with
\[
\max\Re\sigma(A)\approx-0.0789,
\]
but the positive diagonal
\[
D_H\approx\operatorname{diag}(1.58740,1.58740,0.396850)
\]
gives
\[
\max\Re\sigma(D_HA)\approx0.0384>0.
\]
Thus ordinary stability of \(A\) does not imply Hurwitz D-stability, while Theorem~\ref{thm:C10} still certifies the entire orbit as Matignon-stable at this fractional order.

Robustness was examined on fourteen witnesses with local sensitivity, constrained optimization, global differential evolution, directional root finding, and high-precision refinement. The near-boundary witness W28 reaches the fractional boundary after a max-entry perturbation of approximately
\[
1.06\times10^{-3},
\]
while generic witnesses have substantially larger margins. Near \(\alpha=2/3\), where finite differences become ill-conditioned because \(T_\alpha=O(K^{-3})\), the \(500\) worst differential cases were recomputed at 40 digits; the identity
\[
\frac{\partial T_\alpha}{\partial\beta_{ij}}
=
\frac{h_\alpha'(B^*)}{x_k^*}
\]
then agreed to \(2.4\times10^{-33}\) in the worst recheck.
