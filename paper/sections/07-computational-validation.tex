\section{Independent computational validation}
\label{sec:computation}

The results in Sections~\ref{sec:exact-3x3}--\ref{sec:threshold-geometry} are analytic. Finite sampling of positive diagonal multipliers cannot establish D-stability and is never used as proof. Computation was instead organized as an adversarial falsification and certification layer.

\subsection{Independent numerical routes}

Two distinct routes were compared.

\paragraph{Invariant-space route.}
The exact threshold implementation computes
\[
T_\alpha(\beta)
=
\min_{x\in\Delta_2^\circ}
\frac{h_\alpha(B_\beta(x))}{x_1x_2x_3}
\]
using logit coordinates, analytic gradients and Hessians, and high-precision refinement. Selected anchor points are enclosed by interval branch-and-bound.

\paragraph{Direct spectral route.}
Independently of the threshold formula, the code minimizes
\[
\min_{D\succ0}
\left[
\min_{\lambda\in\sigma(DA)}
|\arg\lambda|
-
\frac{\alpha\pi}{2}
\right]
\]
over two log-diagonal ratios. This route uses only eigenvalues of the full matrix \(DA\). The search combined a deterministic log-grid, local pattern/Nelder--Mead refinement, multiple starts, and differential evolution for selected cases.

Agreement between these routes tests the elimination theorem rather than merely retesting one implementation of the same formula.

\subsection{Extreme-scaling pathology and its resolution}

The most serious apparent failures arose from extremely unbalanced diagonal multipliers. When a diagonal ratio reaches \(e^{50}\) or more, one eigenvalue can be tens or hundreds of decimal orders below the spectral radius. Its floating-point sign and argument are then numerically meaningless.

An early unrestricted multiprecision pattern search produced apparent negative margins after escaping to log-diagonal magnitudes as large as approximately \(205\). Those cases were re-evaluated with precision scaled to the magnitude of the diagonal imbalance and with a bounded independent search. The apparent failures disappeared.

A dedicated audit then evaluated \(1{,}350\) strict-P cases at 250 digits over diagonal ratios up to \(10^{60}\) in twelve extreme corner patterns. It produced
\[
0
\]
negative margins; the smallest observed margin was approximately
\[
1.6\times10^{-4}.
\]
This exercise is important methodologically: eigenvalue angles should not be trusted when the relevant eigenvalue is far below the spectral radius unless precision is increased accordingly.

\subsection{Certified threshold anchors}

Sixty representative values of \(T_\alpha(\beta)\) were enclosed using outward-rounded interval arithmetic. The certified relative bracket widths were of order
\[
10^{-39}.
\]
The certification uses:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a boundary-strip exclusion from the divergence of the objective as \(x_1x_2x_3\to0\);
\item an interval-positive-definite Hessian box around the high-precision minimizer;
\item branch-and-bound with monotonic and mean-value interval estimates elsewhere.
\end{enumerate}
These certified anchors test not only numerical optimization but the claimed global minimum.

\subsection{Additional independent checks}

The same computational campaign produced:
\begin{itemize}
\item \(121/121\) passing repository tests;
\item \(8{,}100/8{,}100\) independently generated ecological matrices whose direct spectral classification agreed with the exact C-10/C-13 phase regions;
\item \(210{,}000\) \((\beta,\alpha)\) pairs with no violation of the soundness inequality for Corollary~\ref{cor:Phi-certificate};
\item high-precision verification of the first-order classical-limit rate in Theorem~\ref{thm:C14} down to \(1-\alpha=10^{-10}\);
\item agreement of the symmetric formula
\[
T_\alpha(1,1,1)=1+R_3(\alpha)^3
\]
to approximately \(10^{-107}\) relative scale in high precision.
\end{itemize}

The final computational verdict was therefore
\[
\boxed{\texttt{COMPUTE\_PASS}},
\]
with zero genuine C-10 discrepancies.

\subsection{Reproducibility and evidence levels}

All scripts, seeds, compact result files, worst-case matrices, environment metadata, and interval-certified anchors are stored in the public repository. Numerical outputs are explicitly separated into three evidence levels:
\begin{description}
\item[NUMERICAL] standard floating-point computation;
\item[HP] arbitrary-precision computation;
\item[CERTIFIED] outward-rounded interval enclosure or an exact symbolic identity.
\end{description}

The logical role of this section is deliberately secondary:
\[
\text{analytic proof}
\Longrightarrow
\text{theorem},
\]
whereas
\[
\text{computation}
\Longrightarrow
\text{falsification attempt, corroboration, or certification of examples}.
\]
No theorem in the paper is inferred from Monte Carlo frequency.


\subsection{Code and data availability}

The complete computational record is maintained in the public repository
\[
\text{\url{https://github.com/maximilianolucius/fractional-d-stability-networks}}.
\]
Reusable numerical routines are stored under \texttt{src/fdsn/}; reproduction scripts under \texttt{computations/scripts/}; compact numerical outputs, high-precision rechecks, interval-certified anchors and worst-case matrices under \texttt{computations/results/}; and the independent proof and novelty audits under \texttt{research/}. Raw multi-million-case arrays are not required to reproduce the reported conclusions; their checksums and deterministic generation seeds are recorded in the compact result files.

The repository commit corresponding to the submitted manuscript will be frozen and recorded in the final version. The computational evidence remains logically separate from the analytic proofs.
