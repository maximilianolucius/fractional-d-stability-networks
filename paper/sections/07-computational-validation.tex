\section{Independent computational validation}

The analytic results do not rely on finite diagonal sampling. Computation is used only for adversarial falsification, certification of representative threshold values, and reproducible illustrations.

The final validation campaign consisted of:
\begin{itemize}
\item 121 repository tests, all passing;
\item 2,060,000 C-10 matrix/order cases generated by four mechanisms and multiple boundary-focused families;
\item 120,000 control cases;
\item 56,234 flagged near-boundary or numerically delicate cases re-evaluated at high precision;
\item an independent direct spectral optimization over positive diagonal ratios using eigenvalues of the full matrix \(DA\), separate from the C-10 threshold optimizer;
\item 1,350 strict-P extreme-scaling audits at 250 digits;
\item 60 interval-certified values of \(T_\alpha\), with relative enclosure width approximately \(10^{-39}\);
\item 8,100 direct validations of ecological phase-region classifications.
\end{itemize}

No genuine discrepancy with C-10 survived high-precision re-evaluation:
\[
\boxed{\text{GENUINE\_C10\_COUNTEREXAMPLES}=0.}
\]

The campaign also exposed a numerical pathology relevant for reproducibility: at extremely unbalanced diagonal scalings, eigenvalues many orders of magnitude below the spectral radius can acquire meaningless floating-point angles. The final protocol therefore either bounds the log-diagonal search or increases arithmetic precision with the scaling magnitude.

All scripts, seeds, compact result files, interval certificates and worst-case examples are included in the repository. The numerical layer corroborates the proofs but is not used as proof of D-stability.
