## Reviewer 1

**R1-1 — Cubic boundary factorization.** The revised proof explicitly factors the cubic at the Matignon boundary and identifies its conjugate boundary pair and negative third eigenvalue. **Section 4, p. 7, lines 227–241.**

**R1-2 — Threshold attainment.** Coercivity at the simplex boundary is proved for fractional orders, with a separate reciprocal-coordinate argument at α = 1. **Section 4.2, p. 8, lines 251–260.**

**R1-3 — Strict order monotonicity.** The attained optimizer provides the strict three-term comparison after minimization. **Theorem 5, p. 13, lines 411–426.**

**R1-4 — α = 2/3 endpoint.** One strict Kellogg sector inequality proves |arg λ| > π/3 ≥ απ/2, including the endpoint. The redundant repetition was removed. **Corollary 1, p. 10, lines 327–333.**

**R1-5 — Set versus interior.** A standalone Remark states T₁(β) ≤ κ < Tα(β) for the strict-P fractional-only set and T₁(β) < κ < Tα(β) for its full-dimensional interior, including the imaginary-pair equality case. **Remark 1, p. 10, lines 334–340.**

**R1-6 — Nonlinear Jacobian.** The manuscript distinguishes B, J = diag(u*)B and R*J = D*B, and specifies that the nonlinear comparison uses positive response-rate scaling. **Sections 7.2–7.3, p. 24, lines 736–741; p. 25, lines 748–757.**

**R1-7 — Interval certification.** The outward-rounded mpmath.iv method, working precision, localized minimizer and validated lower threshold enclosure are described. The printed precision of m₀ is distinguished from the high-precision computation; unexplained figure labels were removed. **Section 7.1, p. 23, lines 686–714.**
