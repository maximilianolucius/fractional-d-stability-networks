## Reviewer 2

**R2-1 / R2-2 (minor wording).** Corrected the missing comma after “Therefore” and checked the “consistent with” wording. Locations: sections 03 and 07.

**R2-3 (scope of strict-P condition; matrices with zero principal minors).** We added a paragraph after the principal-minor necessity lemma. It makes three points. (i) Matrices with a vanishing signed principal minor lie in algebraic hypersurfaces; these strata are closed, have Lebesgue measure zero, and contain no interior point of `F_alpha^(3)`. The characterization of the full-dimensional interior is therefore complete as stated. (ii) The strata are not free of fractional-only matrices. Embedding the two-dimensional class (zero diagonal, `bc<0`) as `A = [[0,b,0],[c,0,0],[0,0,-1]]` gives, for every positive diagonal `D`, a purely imaginary pair and a negative eigenvalue. Hence `A` is Matignon stable for every `alpha<1` but is not Hurwitz D-stable, although `p_1=p_2=0`. One therefore cannot claim that nothing happens on these strata. (iii) The four-invariant method does not extend directly to them because its normalization degenerates: `x_i = p_i d_i / a_D` vanishes identically when `p_i=0`, `beta_I = m_I / prod p_i` is undefined, and `m_I=0` deletes a monomial from the orbit coefficients. A classification of these lower-dimensional strata would require separate face-by-face limiting arguments, and we state explicitly that it is not attempted. We deliberately added no numerical experiments for this point: a numerical scan can neither establish nor rule out an extension of an exact all-diagonal characterization. Location: section 04, paragraph "Vanishing principal minors".

**R2-4 (biological feasibility under perturbation of parameters and initial conditions).** We strengthened both parts of the answer.

*Parameters.* The implicit-function argument now comes with an explicit, certified radius. With the same outward-rounded interval arithmetic used for the m-branch, all 14 biological parameters were varied independently in [p_i(1-delta), p_i(1+delta)] around the published W2-A values, with e_1 and e_3 varied separately. For delta = 0.5% (199 subdivision boxes), and also for delta = 0.75% (28,802 boxes), every box is certified to contain a unique prey root with X, Y, Z > 0, m < X < K and s > 0, hence a strict-P reduced matrix. Every box also satisfies both strict inequalities kappa - T_1(beta) > 0 and T_0.9(beta) - kappa > 0. This replaces "a sufficiently small neighbourhood" with an explicit certified one. Location: section 07, paragraph "Certified 14-parameter neighborhood", and section 08.

*Initial conditions.* The previous wording, "locally in time by continuous dependence", was weaker than what holds, and we have replaced it. The vector field is C^1 near u*, and at every parameter vector of the certified box the Jacobian R J is Matignon stable for every positive response-rate matrix R. The linearized asymptotic stability theorem for Caputo systems (Cong, Doan, Siegmund and Tuan, Electron. J. Qual. Theory Differ. Equ. 2016, No. 39, Theorem 3.1) therefore applies. Every sufficiently small perturbation of u* stays in any prescribed neighbourhood of u*, and hence stays positive, for all t >= 0, and converges to u*. The theorem does not quantify the admissible radius. We therefore add, and label explicitly as numerical evidence rather than proof, an integration of 1024 random relative perturbations at each of 20 radii at alpha = 0.9. With the case-study configuration R_*, all trajectories remained positive and returned to the equilibrium for relative radius up to 0.35. With the unscaled model, the corresponding radius was 0.15. The trajectories that failed to return approached the extinction state of the strong Allee effect. No new figure or table was added. Location: section 07, paragraph "Initial-condition perturbations", and section 08.
