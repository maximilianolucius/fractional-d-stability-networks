# Q1 target-journal strategy

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Primary target:** SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications (SIMAX)  
**Status:** provisional until Wave 2 figures and final page count are available.

## Why SIMAX is the primary target

The manuscript's center of gravity is matrix analysis:

- positive-diagonal multiplicative stability;
- exact real 3x3 invariant-space characterization;
- principal-minor orbit coordinates;
- strict convexity of the threshold optimization;
- exact relation to Cain/Bahl-Cain;
- applications to systems/control and mathematical biology.

This matches SIMAX's stated scope: matrix/tensor theory, analysis, applications and computation, including systems/control, mathematical biology and graph theory.

## Submission constraints to design around

Current SIMAX author instructions state:

- one-paragraph abstract, no more than 250 words;
- keywords and MSC codes required;
- figures must be embedded inline;
- SIAM macros strongly encouraged;
- nominal page-limit policy: 20 pages; longer papers can be considered but are harder to referee;
- reproducibility information and repository/supplementary materials are encouraged.

## Manuscript strategy

The current proof-complete technical v1 is intentionally longer than a submission draft.

After Wave 2:

1. convert to SIAM journal class;
2. retain in main text:
   - C-07 theorem;
   - C-10 theorem and its proof;
   - P0/interior theorem;
   - Cain recovery;
   - core C-15 convexity theorem;
   - C-09 dimension theorem;
   - ecological interpretation;
3. compress or move to supplement/appendix:
   - detailed interval-certification protocol;

   - extended proof algebra for some supporting corollaries if page pressure remains;
   - additional witness tables;
4. keep the paper theorem-first: computation should validate and illustrate rather than dominate;
5. target approximately 20--25 SIAM pages in the final formatted version, accepting a modest overrun only if the exact proof package requires it.

## Fallback journal

Linear Algebra and its Applications is an excellent topical fallback because of the direct Cain/Bahl-Cain lineage and its matrix-theory scope. However, if the objective is unambiguously current Q1 in the relevant applied-mathematics category, SIMAX is presently the stronger first target.
