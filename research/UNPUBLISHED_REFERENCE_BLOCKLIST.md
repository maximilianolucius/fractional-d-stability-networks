## Rule for the manuscript

No `arXiv`, `eprint`, `archivePrefix`, `@unpublished`, "submitted", "preprint", "working paper" or "personal communication" may appear in `paper/references.bib`. `computations/reference_audit/reference_audit.py` enforces this on the candidate file and verifies that `paper/references.bib` is the required header followed by the exact candidate contents.
