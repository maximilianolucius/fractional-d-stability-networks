# Research status

## Central object

For a graph-indexed family

`D_C^alpha x = F_G(x; theta)`

study the equilibrium Jacobian `J_G = D F_G(x*)` and the interaction between:

1. Matignon sector stability;
2. first-order/Hurwitz instability;
3. positive diagonal rescaling `D J_G`;
4. network topology and graph motifs;
5. ecological feasibility/admissibility constraints.

## Claim discipline

Use the following labels in research notes and manuscript drafts:

- **PROVED** — complete mathematical proof checked independently.
- **CONDITIONAL** — proof valid under explicit assumptions not yet discharged.
- **CONJECTURE** — mathematically plausible but unproved.
- **COMPUTE-PASS** — survived the stated numerical tests.
- **COMPUTE-FAIL** — counterexample or numerical failure found.
- **OPEN** — unresolved.
