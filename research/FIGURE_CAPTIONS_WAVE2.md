**FIG-05 — Ecological architectures: no-go versus signed-cycle mechanism.** Reduced matrix B = DF at a positive equilibrium drawn as a signed digraph (arrow j → i with sign ± is a nonzero entry a_{ij}). (b) Intraguild predation (z eats y): the two oriented 3-cycles have opposite signs, L₃ < 0 when e₁e₃ > e₂, and κ > T₁ becomes reachable. Theorem diagram (DA-02, DA-03, C-13).

**FIG-06 — The Allee threshold m drives the community through exact invariant space.** Caputo IGP model at α = 0.9, design W2-A (all parameters fixed except m; values in the metadata). (a) Along the coexistence branch, the exact Cain gap κ − T₁ (dashed orange) and the fractional margin T_{0.9} − κ (solid blue); κ − T₁ changes sign exactly once, at m₀ = 0.37445… (60-digit root; circle); square: a classical point (m = 0.19); diamonds: the certified anchor m = 0.511; the strict-P branch ends at m = 0.613 where s → 0⁺ (arrow; the gap curve is shown up to four times its anchor value). Takeaway: raising the Allee threshold alone carries the community from classical to fractional-only robust stability.

Takeaway: in two dimensions fractional-only D-stability is exceptional; in three dimensions it fills an open region.
