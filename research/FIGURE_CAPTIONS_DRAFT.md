# Figure captions — draft (Figure Wave 1, 2026-09-26)

Palette semantics in every figure: blue `#1F4E79` = fractional / Matignon; orange `#D97A00` = classical / Cain / Hurwitz; green `#2A7F62` = biologically admissible / realized; red `#B14E3A` = excluded / unstable; solid = exact boundary, dashed = classical comparator or asymptotic, squares/diamonds = certified points, dotted = auxiliary guide.

**FIG-01 — Matignon sector versus Hurwitz half-plane.** Complex eigenvalue plane for (a) a generic order 0<α<1 (drawn with α=0.75) and (b) α=0.9. Orange: the classical Hurwitz half-plane Re z<0. Blue rays: the exact Matignon boundary |arg z|=απ/2; the blue sliver απ/2<|arg z|≤π/2, Re z≥0 is the set of eigenvalue locations that are admissible for the Caputo system of order α but not for the integer-order system. Every element is an exact theorem object (Matignon's criterion, C-01); no numerical approximation is involved. Takeaway: fractional-only stability is a sector-versus-half-plane difference, and the sliver closes as α→1.

**FIG-02 — Dimension two versus dimension three.** (a) For the 2×2 reduced matrix B₂ of the two-species double-Allee model (a₂₂=0, det B₂=pq>0), the exact classification C-07 gives: fractional D-stability iff a₁₁=g'_{DA}(X)≤0, classical D-stability iff a₁₁<0; the fractional-only set is the line a₁₁=0 (blue), a codimension-one locus, so the difference class has empty interior. (b) On the symmetric slice β=(b,b,b) of the 3×3 invariant space (α=0.9) the exact C-10 band T₁(b,b,b)=9b<κ<T_α(b,b,b)=27h_α(b/3) (blue area between the dashed Cain boundary and the solid fractional boundary, both closed-form) is a nonempty open set (C-09/C-10, C-15 Cor. 1). Takeaway: dimension three is the first dimension with robust fractional-only D-stability.

**FIG-03 — Exact C-10 threshold geometry.** (a) Symmetric slice β=(b,b,b): the closed-form boundaries T₁=9b (dashed orange) and T_α(b,b,b)=27h_α(b/3) for α=0.7, 0.8, 0.9 (blue); the shaded band is the genuinely fractional region at α=0.9. (b) Asymmetric slice β=(β₁₂,1.5,3) at α=0.9: T₁=(√β₁₂+√1.5+√3)² (closed form) and the exact C-10 threshold T_{0.9}(β)=min_x h_α(B_β(x))/(x₁x₂x₃) evaluated at its unique minimiser (C-15); squares are interval-certified values of T_{0.9} at β₁₂∈{0.2,1,5} (bracket widths below 10⁻³⁰). No positive-diagonal sampling is used anywhere. Takeaway: the exact fractional boundary lies strictly above Cain's classical boundary and the gap between them is the open band of C-10.

**FIG-04 — Deformation of the exact threshold with α.** (a) T_α(β)/T₁(β) on 2/3<α<1 for three pair-loop triples (exact C-10 evaluation); the ratio decreases monotonically to 1 (C-14 Thm 1) and diverges as α↓2/3. (b) Classical limit: T_α−T₁ versus 1−α (40-digit evaluation) against the C-14 asymptotic C(β)(1−α) (dashed), C=π(S^{5/2}/√G+S^{3/2}√G). (c) Low-order limit: T_αK³/27 versus K=1−4cos²(απ/2) (60-digit evaluation) against the C-15 Theorem 3 expansion 1+K(9Σβ−27)/27 (dashed). Solid curves are numerical evaluations of the exact variational formula; dashed lines are closed-form asymptotics. Takeaway: the memory-induced band closes linearly at the classical end and opens like 27/K³ at the low-order end, with explicit motif-dependent coefficients.

**FIG-05 — Ecological architectures: no-go versus signed-cycle mechanism.** Network diagrams of the reduced matrix B=DF at a positive equilibrium; an arrow j→i with sign ± is a nonzero entry a_{ij} (effect of species j on the per-capita growth of species i). (a) Two consumers competing for a double-Allee prey: both oriented three-cycle products are positive, so L₃>0 and κ=Σβ−2−L₃<T₁(β) at every strict-P equilibrium — the open fractional-only band is unreachable (no-go theorem). (b) Intraguild predation (z eats y): the two oriented three-cycles have opposite signs, L₃=hq₁q₂(e₂−e₁e₃)/(sc₁c₂)<0 when e₁e₃>e₂, and κ can exceed T₁. Theorem diagram; no data are plotted. Takeaway: the sign structure of the directed three-cycles, not the number of species, decides whether the fractional-only band is accessible.

**FIG-06 — The Allee threshold m drives the model through exact invariant space.** Caputo IGP model at α=0.9 with all parameters fixed except m (values in the metadata). (a) Along the coexistence branch, the exact Cain gap G₁(m)=κ−T₁ (dashed orange) and the fractional margin G_α(m)=T_α−κ (solid blue); G₁ changes sign exactly once, at m₀=0.2 (60-digit root; circle), with a classical point at m=0.12 (square) and the certified interior anchor at m=0.35 (diamonds); the branch loses feasibility at m≈0.643. (b) The same path in the invariant slice β₁₂=β₁₃=1+A₀t, β₂₃=C₀=2, κ=C₀+(A₀+B₀+E₀)t with t=1/s(m): a straight line (green) crossing the exact Cain boundary transversally and moving monotonically deeper into the band between T₁ (dashed) and T_{0.9} (solid). All curves are exact formulas evaluated along the branch; the crossing is located to 60 digits. Takeaway: increasing the Allee threshold alone moves the community from classical robust stability into fractional-only robust stability, transversally and once.

**FIG-07 — Certified biological anchor at m=0.35.** Same model as FIG-06. (a) Coexistence densities (X,Y,Z) along m; (b) the two invariant margins κ−T₁ and T_{0.9}−κ with interval-certified lower bounds at m∈{0.30,0.33,0.35,0.38,0.40} (squares; interval Newton and outward rounding at 50 digits); (c) spectrum of D*B at the worst positive diagonal found by a scale-invariant search (minimum eigenvalue angle over D): the conjugate pair has |arg λ|=1.539>0.9π/2 but Re λ>0, i.e. the equilibrium is Matignon-stable for every positive diagonal scaling yet not Hurwitz D-stable; (d) linearised Caputo dynamics at that diagonal (predictor–corrector scheme): decay for α=0.9, growth for α=1. Panels (a,b) are exact-formula evaluations with certified markers; (c,d) are numerical corroboration. Takeaway: a biologically feasible equilibrium with comfortable certified margins realizes the exact fractional-only class.

**FIG-08 — Exact 2D/3D double-Allee contrast.** (a) Two-species model: in the (X,m) plane (a=0.5, K=1.1) the fractional-only mechanism is the single curve m=m_c(X)=(X²+2aX−Ka)/(K+a), i.e. g'_{DA}(X)=0 (blue): classical D-stability below, instability above — codimension one. (b) Three-species IGP model at α=0.9: the (m,K) slice of the biological parameter space classified pointwise by the exact C-10 criterion (coexistence solved exactly, T_α = exact C-10 minimum): classical (orange), fractional-only (blue, open), infeasible or non-strict-P (grey); the green segment is the interval-certified m-interval [0.2005,0.45] at K=1.1 and the diamond the FIG-07 anchor. Takeaway: in two dimensions fractional-only D-stability is exceptional; in three dimensions it fills an open region of parameter space.
