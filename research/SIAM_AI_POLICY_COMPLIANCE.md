# SIAM generative AI policy compliance

**Verified:** 2026-09-25  
**Policy version:** SIAM Publications Editorial Policy on Artificial Intelligence, Version 2.0, effective May 2026.

For uses beyond routine grammar checking, SIAM requires disclosure in an Acknowledgements or Declarations section and a statement confirming author responsibility for all content.

The manuscript therefore includes:

\`paper/sections/10-declarations.tex\`

Current disclosure:

> Generative AI tools, including OpenAI ChatGPT and Anthropic Claude, were used to assist with drafting and editing, organization of literature searches, mathematical proof exploration and consistency checking, development and review of symbolic and numerical code, and preparation of reproducibility materials. These tools are not authors. The author assumes responsibility for all content.

This wording is intentionally factual and does not claim that every item has already undergone a separate human verification step beyond the author's responsibility.

Before external submission, the human author should personally review and be prepared to defend every theorem, proof, citation, computation and figure, as required by SIAM policy.
