# Internal supporting material only

This directory may contain derivations, scratch proofs, robustness checks, tables, or other internal research support.

It is **not** intended to be a required journal supplement. Under the current publication directives, every load-bearing theorem, proof dependency, numerical specification, and conclusion must fit inside the self-contained main article (maximum 25 pages). Material kept here is for internal validation, archival support, or later reuse only.
